GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between A+ Educators, Scalers, and Thrivers?

A+ Educators is our free, permanent community — webinars, newsletter, resource library, and more. Scalers and Thrivers are paid upgrade tiers within Profitable Educators Nexus, adding expert-led training, accountability, and (for Thrivers) 1-on-1 support and brand-building resources.

How do I join A+ Educators for free?

Click any "Join A+ Educators" button on this page — it takes you to a short Google Form. Once submitted, you will be added to the community directly.

What happens after I pay for Thrivers?

After your $10 deposit, you will receive confirmation and next steps by email. Once your $20 balance is paid by September 30th, you are fully onboarded with your welcome pack and first session details.

Is $30 a one-time payment?

No — Thrivers membership runs in 3-month cycles. $30 is the founding-member rate for your first quarter if you join by September 12th. After that, the regular rate of $50 per quarter applies going forward.

What happens when my 3 months are up?

You will get a renewal reminder before your membership expires. If you do not renew right away, you are simply moved back to free A+ Educators access — no penalty, and you can rejoin Thrivers anytime.
